SKYBOUND is a 3D tower obby built with Rojo and Luau: climb 14 colourful stages from the grass plaza to the golden pad at the top, grabbing 10 spinning coins on the way. Moving and vanishing platforms, lava and kill bricks, checkpoints every four stages, a live HUD (stage, coins, timer) and a win screen with Play Again. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best Roblox game” bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl
```

Then open `game.rbxl` in Roblox Studio, or play the published experience at the link in `PLAY.json`.
